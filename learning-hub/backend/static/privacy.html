McPerry Learning Hub

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

McPerry Learning Hub is a BECE and SHS learning service operated for controlled school and teacher pilots.

Information we use

Administrators create student and teacher accounts using a name and email address. Passwords are stored as one-way password hashes. The service stores question attempts, progress totals, reports, teacher content, and administrator audit records so the learning and review workflows can operate.

Working notes, bookmarks, and cached explanations are stored in the learner's browser or device. They are not sent to the server unless the learner submits an attempt or report. If AI explanations are enabled, the selected question text, subject, topic, and options are sent to the configured AI provider; passwords, names, and email addresses are not sent in that request.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, deliver assigned learning content, show aggregate usage statistics, review teacher submissions, respond to question reports, protect the service from abuse, and improve educational materials. We do not sell personal information and the pilot contains no advertising.

Sharing and storage

School administrators and assigned teachers receive only the access permitted by their role. Teacher usage views are limited to aggregate activity for assigned topics and questions. Data is stored by the school or service operator on the configured server and may be backed up by the administrator. Uploaded lesson videos are served only after publication.

Retention and choices

The administrator can disable accounts and remove or archive learning content. Ask the school administrator to correct or delete account data. Learners can sign out to clear local catalog, bookmark, solution, and working-note data from that device.

Children and schools

This service is intended for use under school, teacher, or parent/guardian supervision. Administrators should obtain any required consent and configure access for their learners before use.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the administrator who provided your McPerry Learning Hub account.
