McPerry Learning Hub

Account and data deletion

Effective date: 11 October 2026

To request deletion of a McPerry Learning Hub account and associated server-held personal data, email mcperryimaginations@gmail.com from the account email address.

Include in your request

  • The learner or teacher name on the account
  • The account email address
  • The school or administrator who issued the account, if known

What is deleted

After the request is verified, the administrator will disable the account and delete or anonymize account-related learning records that are not required for school safeguarding, legal, security, or audit obligations. Local browser or device data can be removed by signing out and clearing the app or browser data.

Timing and exceptions

Requests are reviewed by the service administrator. Some records may be retained where required for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or school safeguarding. The administrator will explain any retained data in the response.
