# McPerry Learning Hub privacy policy

The publishable HTML version is available at `/privacy` when the backend is hosted. Before Google Play submission, replace the site URL in the Play listing with the public HTTPS URL for this page and confirm that the operator contact path is current.

The policy covers account identity, hashed passwords, question attempts, progress, reports, teacher content, audit records, local browser notes/bookmarks, optional AI processing, administrator access, retention, school supervision, and deletion requests. It intentionally makes no advertising or sale-of-data claims beyond the current application behavior.
